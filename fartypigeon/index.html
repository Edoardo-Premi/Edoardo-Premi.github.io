Farty Pigeon

Our pigeon can't flap its wings… so it flies by farting!

Android iPhone

Screenshots

How it plays

Three buttons

PUFF for a small push up, MEGA for a big one when you're in trouble, FIRE to shoot. Holding does nothing: every tap counts once.

Ten silly bosses

Sergeant Hawk, the Pirate Gull, Rocket Cat, the Abductor UFO and more. Beat them to keep flying.

Ten places

The city park, the old town, skyscrapers, the airport, the coast, the countryside, the mountains, the city at night, a storm and the stratosphere.

Coins and weapons

Earn coins by playing and unlock 9 weapons, from the egg and the homing seeds to the beak laser.

Good to know

  • Free to play. An ad appears every few games, only on the game over screen, never while you fly. Ads are never personalised.
  • Optional videos let you keep playing once after a crash, or double the coins of a game. It's always your choice.
  • Optional in-app purchases: "No ads" and cosmetic items. Nothing you buy makes you stronger, and coins are never sold.
  • No account and no sign-in. Your progress stays on your device.
  • In English, Italian, German, French and Spanish.
